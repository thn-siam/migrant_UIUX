## Brand & Style

This design system channels radiant hospitality, dynamic civic empowerment, and communal warmth. Built to support individuals and families navigating complex civic, legal, and community transitions, the aesthetic completely removes sterile bureaucratic chill in favor of an inviting, optimistic hearth. The interface evokes safe shelter, active solidarity, and heartfelt dignity while maintaining impeccable institutional precision.

The design expression fuses **Empathetic Humanism** with **Vibrant Civic Energy**:
- **Radiant Warmth:** Amber and energetic orange anchors project vitality, welcome, and hope without sacrificing authority.
- **Radical Clarity:** Strict contrast structures, unambiguous interactions, and uncluttered viewports protect users operating under acute cognitive fatigue or language barriers.
- **Tactile Trust:** Warm neutral planes, balanced corner geometry, and deliberate visual affirmations build deep psychological comfort between community members, caseworkers, and volunteers.
- **Uncompromised Accessibility:** Every interactive surface, badge, and structural state conforms strictly to WCAG AAA contrast and touch target standards.

## Colors

The color architecture replaces the cool teal foundation with a warm, energetic amber-orange system. To guarantee WCAG AAA compliance (7:1 contrast for regular text on light surfaces), the primary brand color is calibrated to `#C2410C` (Rust Orange 700) for standard textual elements and interactive button fills against light containers, while `#EA580C` and `#F97316` function as vibrant accent highlights, icon keys, and expressive hero elements.

- **Primary (`#C2410C` / `#EA580C` - Warm Energetic Amber-Orange):** Represents warmth, civic vitality, and community shelter. Interactive primary actions utilize `#C2410C` with pure white text to achieve a >7:1 contrast ratio.
- **Secondary (`#0369A1` - Trust Marine Blue):** Provides a balancing cool counterweight for institutional validation, legal resources, and navigation affordances, ensuring warmth does not become overwhelming or ambiguous.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309` - Deep Amber Ochre):** Used for advisory notices, secondary category indicators, and reflective community metadata.
- **Neutral (`#43464A` - Warm Slate Neutral):**
  - Base Canvas: `#FCF9F6` (a gentle, warm-tinted alabaster that avoids clinical white glare)
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` for elevated modular cards
  - High-Emphasis Content: `#1C1917` (Stone 900)
  - Medium-Emphasis Body: `#44403C` (Stone 700)
  - Subtle Framing Borders: `#E7E5E4` (Stone 200) and `#D6D3D1` (Stone 300)
- **Status & Safety Signifiers:**
  - Verified Identity & Trust: `#14532D` on `#F0FDF4` (bordered by `#86EFAC`)
  - Resource Pending / Attention: `#78350F` on `#FFFBEB` (bordered by `#FCD34D`)
  - Crisis / Urgent Mutual Aid: `#881337` on `#FFF1F2` (bordered by `#FDA4AF`)

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is specified across all typographic scales. The font's geometric clarity, humanized curve joins, and open counters provide unmatched legibility for multilingual readers, translation overlays, and assistive visual tooling.

- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy leverages spacious line-heights (`1.625x` on `body-md`) to ensure uninterrupted tracking across complex multilingual text blocks.
- **Structure Over Grandiosity:** Headings emphasize structural clarity and warm authority via `600` and `700` weights rather than immense scale.
- **Scannability:** Microcopy, badge indicators, and interface tags use `label-sm` and `label-md` with slight positive tracking to maintain clear letter separation even on smaller mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial relationships are rendered through clean planar tiers and delicate warm-tinted drop shadows, avoiding cold grays or stark skeuomorphic drop-offs.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat, soothing warm tone (`#FCF9F6`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Static Containers):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, bounded by a 1px solid border (`#E7E5E4`), backed by a warm ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Buddy Tiles):** Elevated with warm amber diffusion: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(68, 64, 60, 0.04)`, bordered by `#FDBA74`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Assistance Drawers & Floating Dialogs):** High planar elevation: `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)` paired with a semi-opaque warm backdrop scrim (`#1C1917` at 45% opacity with 3px blur).

## Shapes

The design system embraces a roundedness level of `2` (0.5rem base radius), projecting approachability, safety, and modern civic dignity.

- **Base Components:** Form fields, resource modules, alerts, and action buttons adopt `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Major Surfaces:** Welcome cards, community spotlight headers, and modal dialogs use `1rem` (16px / `rounded-lg`).
- **Affordance & Pills:** Language tags, verification indicators, and category filter chips adopt full circular capsules (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to invite direct fingertip interaction.

- **Warm Subtle / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #D6D3D1`, text `#1C1917`. Hover: background `#FFF7ED`, border `#FDBA74`, text `#C2410C`.

- Includes volunteer/buddy avatar, online status indicator, language proficiency chips, location distance, and a prominent warm CTA button ("Connect with [Name]").
